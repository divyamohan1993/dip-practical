CSU2543

Digital Image Processing Lab

8 interactive practicals covering Gonzalez & Woods 3rd Edition. Shoolini University.

01 G&W Ch 2 Loading & Displaying Images Load digital images, inspect properties, display histograms, and compare multiple images. Explore → 02 G&W Ch 2 Impact of Sampling Rate on Spatial Resolution Progressive downsampling to analyze sampling rate and spatial resolution. Explore → 03 G&W Ch 2–3 Negation, Subtraction & Inversion Image negation, absolute difference subtraction, and intensity inversion with medical imaging. Explore → 04 G&W Ch 3 Gamma Correction & Power Law Transformations Power law transformations, log transforms, and contrast enhancement for dark and bright images. Explore → 05 G&W Ch 3 Histogram Equalization Enhance contrast via CDF-based intensity redistribution, transfer functions, and PDF/CDF analysis. Explore → 06 G&W Ch 3 Histogram Matching & Specification Transform image histograms to match target distributions via CDF inversion and multi-target comparison. Explore → 07 G&W Ch 3 2D Correlation & Convolution Implement correlation and convolution from first principles, demonstrate the impulse-response distinction, and apply standard kernels to a real image. Explore → 08 G&W Ch 3 Spatial Filtering: Smoothing & Sharpening Box and median filters for noise smoothing; Laplacian and Sobel operators for sharpening and edge detection. Explore →
